# Cabin Tape

A small pixel-art firefly game for winding down before sleep. Catch fireflies in six night places (Deer Woods, Castle Hearth, Sunflower Field, The Water Works, Lake Simcoe, Snow Cabin) while a warm tape-warbled drone, a little music-box tune and synthesized field recordings play.

**Play:** https://hamidmvlek.github.io/cabin-tape/

- Tap glowing fireflies, or drag through them.
- Hold your finger still to call them closer.
- Fill a jar with twelve to wake something up in that place.
- The ☾ menu has places, a sleep timer, and volume, music and field-sound levels.

Everything, graphics and sound, is generated live in the browser from one `index.html`. On iPhone, open it in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app.
